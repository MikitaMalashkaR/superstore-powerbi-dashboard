# Power BI dashboard for Superstore sales analysis (2014-2017)

## Overview

This dashboard visualises four years of sales data (2014–2017) for a US-based retail company selling Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology products.

## Key Findings

- The data indicates a steady growth in sales over the 4-year period, with a notable acceleration in 2017 (sales increased from $484K in 2014 to $733K in 2017 — a ~51% growth).
- The West region leads in total sales with $725K, followed closely by the East at $678K. Central region contributes $501K, while the South significantly underperforms at $391K — a 46% gap compared to the West leader.
- Sales by all the categories are relatively evenly distributed, but profit margins in Technology and Office Supplies (17.4% and 17%) are almost 7 times higher than in Furniture (2.5%).
- Discounts for the Furniture category slightly affect the profit because the profit for this category is always nearly 0. In contrast, discounts for Office supplies and Technology have a strong impact on profit.
